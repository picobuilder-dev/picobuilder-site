About PicoBuilder

Many small businesses in South Africa are missing out on customers because they do not have a presence online.

Studies have shown that a significant percentage of owners of small businesses, consultants,  freelancers, or one-man operations that are typically run on tight budgets believe that a website is a luxury.  


At the same time, there’s a common belief that a proper website needs to be large, complex, and filled with multiple pages. For most small, one-person businesses, that’s not the case at all. An elaborate website for a one-man show with the bells and whistles may be impressive, but in most cases, it's overkill.


Become visible


Simply put, if your business isn’t visible online today, it is almost invisible. For example, potential customers, whether they hear of your business, service, or product through word of mouth, a chance meeting, or in a conversation, if they are in the market, will almost certainly look for you online. Or ask you for your website address. 

When people search online, even a simple, professional web presence can make the difference between being found and being overlooked.


PicoBuilder was created to solve this.


We focus on building clean, effective one-page scrollable websites that give small businesses a strong online presence without the complexity. Everything is designed to be simple, fast, and affordable — while still looking professional and trustworthy.


Designed and built in South Africa, PicoBuilder gives local businesses something important: a website that is fully their own — not locked into a complicated platform, and not dependent on expensive systems and monthly subscriptions.


Whether you’re a plumber, electrician, landscaper, or a service-based business, PicoBuilder makes it easy to get online with a website that works.
