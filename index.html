About PicoBuilder

Many small businesses in South Africa are missing out on customers because they do not have a presence online. Studies have shown that many owners of small businesses, consultants, freelancers, or one-man operations believe that a website is a luxury.

At the same time, there’s a common belief that a proper website needs to be large, complex, and filled with multiple pages. For most small businesses, that is not necessary.

If your business isn’t visible online today, it is almost invisible. Customers will search for you online first — even from word of mouth.
